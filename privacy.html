Privacy Policy

How Proof AI collects, uses, and protects your data.

Effective Date: March 18, 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (name, email, company, phone), information generated by using Proof (job data, photos, call recordings, documents), and standard technical data (IP address, browser, device type).

2. How We Use It

We use this data to operate and improve the Proof platform, provide customer support, process payments, and communicate with you about your account. Customer data is never used to train third-party AI models.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with service providers necessary to run Proof (e.g. cloud hosting, payment processing, AI processing partners), insurance carriers or TPAs when you choose to submit a claim, and as required by law.

4. Data Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and audit logging to protect your data. Each customer's data is isolated within its own tenant environment.

5. Your Rights

You can access, export, or delete your data at any time through your account, or by contacting us directly.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our contact page or emailed directly to Priority@proofco.ai.
