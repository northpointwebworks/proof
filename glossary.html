Glossary

The field service terms you need to know.

Restoration & insurance

Tap a term to expand.

FNOL+

First Notice of Loss. The initial report filed with an insurance carrier when a property damage claim is made.

TPA+

Third Party Administrator. An organization that processes insurance claims on behalf of carriers.

IICRC+

Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification. The industry body that sets standards for restoration work.

Cat 2+

Category 2 water damage. Contains significant contamination and has the potential to cause discomfort or sickness.

Class 3+

Class 3 water damage. The entire area is affected, including walls, ceilings, and structural materials.

Moisture Mapping+

The process of documenting moisture levels across a structure to track drying progress.

Scope of Work+

A detailed document describing the work to be performed, materials needed, and associated costs.

Evidence Vault+

Proof's secure storage system for job photos, videos, and documentation that supports insurance claims.
