Implications

What this means for you.

The data paints a clear picture — the operators who automate capture, documentation, and dispatch win. Those who don't bleed revenue quietly.

You're losing nearly 3 in 4 night calls

With 73% of after-hours calls hitting voicemail, most restoration companies are surrendering the majority of their emergency pipeline to whoever picks up first. An autonomous answering layer turns that leak into captured revenue — overnight.

Documentation is your hidden bottleneck

4.2 hours per job spent on paperwork is a full workday lost every couple of jobs. When documentation is automated to minutes, your crews spend that time on billable work — and adjusters get clean, compliant files faster.

Every missed call is $2,400 gone

A single missed emergency averages $2,400 in lost revenue — and 31% of claims stall on paperwork before they're ever paid. The compound cost of manual processes quietly dwarfs the price of fixing them.
